
# **README.md for Car Price Prediction App**

**Title:** Car Price Prediction App

**Description:**

The Car Price Prediction App is a web-based application that allows users to predict the price of a car based on its features. The app uses a machine learning model that has been trained on a dataset of car prices.

**Features:**

* The app allows users to input the features of their car, such as the make, model, year, mileage, and fuel type.
* The app uses a machine learning model to predict the price of the car based on the user input.
* The app displays the predicted price of the car to the user.
* The app also provides a link to a Google Sheet where users can view the data that was used to train the machine learning model.

**Target Audience:**

The Car Price Prediction App is intended for anyone who is interested in buying or selling a car. The app can help users to get a better idea of the value of a car before they make a purchase or sale.

**Benefits:**

The Car Price Prediction App can help users to:

* Save money by getting a better deal on a car.
* Make more informed decisions about buying or selling a car.
* Avoid overpaying or underselling a car.

Visit the Car Price Prediction App today to get a free estimate of the value of your car.
